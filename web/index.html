FM-1 Screen Reader

Plug in your FM-1 with a USB cable and connect. Everything the screen shows is read out: pages, knobs as you turn them, messages, help cards, lists and steps.

Connection

Not connected.

The screen

Keys: W the whole screen, A again, S stop speaking, V voice on or off.

Voice

Who reads it

Your screen reader speaks while this window is in front. This page's voice keeps speaking from a background tab, while you play the FM-1.

What it said

    Works in Chrome, Edge and Opera (Web MIDI). The voice is eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0), running in this page. Every FM-1 firmware with screen reader support works here: each says its own words. Add support to a firmware.
