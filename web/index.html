FM-1 Screen Reader

Plug in your FM-1 with a USB cable and connect. Everything the screen shows is read out: pages, knobs as you turn them, messages, help cards, lists and steps.

Connection

Not connected.

Needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, and an FM-1 firmware with screen reader support. When the browser asks to let this page use MIDI devices, allow it.

The FM-1's screen

Keys: W the whole screen, A again, S stop speaking, V voice on or off. They don't work while you type or are on a setting.
